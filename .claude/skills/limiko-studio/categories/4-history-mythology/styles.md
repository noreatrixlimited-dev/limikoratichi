# History and mythology styles

Match the style to the culture and era of the story. Drawn in code with `../../shared/building.md`.
🟢 easy · 🟡 medium · 🔴 heavy.

## Indian traditions
| id | Look | Motion | Sound | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `kalamkari-scroll` 🟡 | Hand-drawn pen outlines, earthy reds, indigo and mustard on cream cloth, dense borders | Scenes unroll like a cloth scroll, lines ink in | Veena, mridangam, temple bells | Ramayana, Mahabharata, Andhra stories |
| `miniature-court` 🔴 | Flat perspective, jewel colours, gold borders, fine faces in profile | Panels open like a book, slow pushes into details | Sitar, tanpura drone | Kings, courts, Mughal and Rajput history |
| `temple-relief` 🔴 | Carved stone panels, deep shadows, warm sandstone | Light sweeps across the carving, camera tracks along the wall | Chanting pads, bells, stone ambience | Puranic stories, temple history |
| `pattachitra-panel` 🟡 | Bold outlines, flat bright fills, decorative borders, stylised large eyes | Panel-to-panel moves, border patterns animate | Folk drums, flute | Krishna and Jagannath stories, folk legends |
| `leather-puppet` 🟡 | Translucent coloured leather puppets lit from behind, perforated patterns | Puppets pivot on joints, flame flicker | Drum, harmonium, narrator | Telugu and South Indian epic retellings |
| `mural-fresco` 🔴 | Faded wall painting, cracked plaster, mineral pigments | Slow pans, light restores colour as the story advances | Ambient cave echo, flute | Ancient history, Buddhist stories |

## History and mythology look rules
- One culture per film; costumes, weapons and architecture match the era.
- Gods and heroes keep their traditional colours and attributes in every shot.
- Grain, paper or stone texture on every frame keeps the world "old".
