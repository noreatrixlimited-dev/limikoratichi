# Kids channel playbook

**Promise:** warm, safe, colourful stories and learning for ages 2–8.

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Length | Shorts 30–60 s · episodes 3–8 min · songs 1.5–3 min |
| Size | 1920×1080 (most kids watch on TV and tablets) plus a vertical cut |
| Styles | `crayon-storybook`, `wet-watercolor`, `pop-up-paper`, `felt-and-yarn`, `toy-bricks`, `tiny-island`, `bendy-cartoon`, `fifties-flat`, `chalk-classroom`, `shadow-theatre` |
| Voice | warm, slow (120–140 wpm), smiling, clear consonants |
| Music | major key, glockenspiel/ukulele/marimba, 90–120 bpm; songs with repetition |
| Characters | one recurring hero with a simple silhouette, 3 colours, a catchphrase |

## Procedure
1. One learning goal (a colour, a number, a feeling, a habit) or one gentle story arc.
2. Script: greeting → small problem → three tries (repetition) → solution → recap → goodbye line.
3. Slow pacing: shots 3–5 s, no flashing, no sudden loud sounds, no scary moments.
4. Every new word appears on screen and is said twice.
5. Lip sync for talking characters: `A-rhubarb` mouth shapes for 2D.
6. Checks before publishing: no flashing over 3 times per second, loudness −16 LUFS, no ads in the story, no links.
7. Upload marked **Made for kids** in YouTube Studio.
8. Package: title uses parent-search words ("Learn shapes with Pip the fox | Kids story").

## Series ideas
Bedtime stories, colour and shape songs, animal sounds, "how things work" for kids, manners, seasons.
