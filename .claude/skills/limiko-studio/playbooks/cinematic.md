# Cinematic shorts playbook

**Promise:** a small, complete story with a strong look.

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Length | 45–90 s |
| Size | 1920×1080 or 2.39:1 letterbox inside 1920×1080; vertical cut for Shorts |
| Styles | `silent-reel`, `spy-title`, `miniature-city`, `oil-impasto`, `woodcut-legend`, `glass-render`, `ink-river`, `anime-cel-80s` |
| Routes | A (code-drawn) for stylised; B (AI clips) when it must look photographic |

## Procedure
1. **Logline**: who wants what, what stops them, how it ends (one sentence).
2. **Beat sheet** (5): image of normal → disruption → struggle → turn → final image that echoes the first.
3. **Storyboard**: 12–20 shots: wide, medium, close; one camera move per shot (push, pan, tilt, orbit).
4. **Sound design first**: ambience bed, then music cue points on the turn and the final image.
5. **Build**, then grade for one dominant colour plus one accent.
6. **Title card** and end card in the style; credits in `credits.txt`.
7. **Check**: watch muted. Does the story still read? If not, fix the pictures, not the narration.
