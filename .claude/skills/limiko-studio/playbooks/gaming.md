# Gaming channel playbook

**Promise:** fast, funny or impressive moments, lore and rankings.

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Length | Shorts 20–45 s · long-form 8–15 min |
| Size | 1080×1920 Shorts (gameplay top, face or graphics bottom) · 1920×1080 long |
| Styles (graphics) | `pixel-quest`, `diorama-2d`, `micro-games`, `quiz-show`, `terminal-crt`, `holo-hud`, `liminal-tape`, `space-sitcom` |
| Voice | high energy, 180–200 wpm, reactions |
| Music | electronic or chiptune 120–150 bpm, cuts on the beat |
| Hook patterns | gaming flex, challenge, result first, countdown (packaging.md #15, #12, #1, #6) |

## Procedure
1. Footage: the user's own gameplay recordings (Route C). Check the game publisher's video policy;
   most allow monetised gameplay with commentary.
2. Find the moment: biggest reaction or result goes in the first second.
3. Pace: median shot 0.8–1.5 s in Shorts; zoom punch-ins on kills, wins, fails.
4. Overlays: kill counters, arrows, freeze frame plus "wait for it", rank graphics in our styles.
5. Sound: game audio −6 dB under voice, a sound effect on every cut, bass drop on payoff.
6. Lore and tier-list videos: Route A with `holo-hud` or `pixel-quest` graphics.

## Series ideas
"Things you missed in…", tier lists, speedrun stories, "1 vs 100", game myths tested.
