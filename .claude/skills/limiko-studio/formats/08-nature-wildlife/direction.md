# 08 · Nature and wildlife: direction profile

**Identity:** calm, beautiful, true to nature. Smooth floating camera, atmospheric light, detailed
animals, gentle transitions, rich environmental sound. Slow motion for beautiful or decisive moments.

## Numbers
| | |
|---|---|
| Median shot | 6 s (4–12 s) |
| Camera | aerial glides over landscapes, slow tracking alongside animals, long-lens close-ups with shallow depth of field, very slow push-ins |
| Animation | realistic, weight and timing from real animal behaviour; secondary motion everywhere (grass, water, fur, breath) |
| Effects max | level 1 (light rays, mist, dust, depth of field); slow motion 0.25–0.5× on key moments |
| Transitions | straight cut, slow cross-dissolve (24–36 f), J-cut with nature sound leading |
| Light | golden hour and blue hour preferred; backlit fur and water; soft haze for depth |
| Sound | layered ambience (wind, water, insects, birds), animal sounds accurate to the species, gentle score; −16 LUFS |

## Shot types
Aerial establishing (landscape, scale) · environment detail (dew, bark, ripples) · animal wide (in habitat) ·
animal medium (behaviour) · macro close-up (eyes, feathers, claws) · behaviour sequence (hunt, feed,
play, migrate) in slow motion at the decisive instant · time-lapse (clouds, flowers opening, tides).

## Routes
- Real footage the user owns or licensed: edit with `../../shared/editing.md`.
- Code-drawn or 3D: stylised nature (`wet-watercolor`, `tiny-island`, `ink-river`, low-poly 3D) for kids and explainers.
- AI clips (`../../shared/ai-models.md`) for photoreal shots; check anatomy and species accuracy frame by frame.

## Styles
| id | Look | For |
|---|---|---|
| `golden-real` | photoreal footage, warm grade, soft contrast | wildlife films |
| `macro-world` | extreme close-ups, creamy bokeh | insects, plants, small animals |
| `wet-watercolor` | painterly washes | gentle nature stories |
| `lowpoly-nature` | faceted 3D landscapes, pastel light | explainers, kids nature |
| `ink-river` | ink wash mountains and mist | poetic landscapes |

## Special planning (G4)
Sequence list per habitat: establishing → animal intro → behaviour sequence → detail → wider context
(range map, season) → closing landscape. Facts about species sourced.

## Packaging
Hooks: the decisive behaviour moment in slow motion, a surprising animal fact, a landscape reveal.
Titles: `The <Animal> That <Surprising Thing>`, `<Habitat>: <N> Days in <Place>`.

## Checklist
- [ ] Species behaviour, sounds and habitats accurate; facts sourced
- [ ] Camera moves smooth and slow; no abrupt effects
- [ ] Every shot has natural motion and sound
- [ ] Slow motion reserved for key moments
