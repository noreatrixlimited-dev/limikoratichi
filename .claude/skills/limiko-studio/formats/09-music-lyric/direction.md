# 09 · Music and lyric videos: direction profile

**Identity:** picture, type, light and camera move with the music. Beat-matched transitions,
rhythmic camera, visual storytelling. The song is the star; nothing competes with it.

> **Moving-camera rule:** the camera moves in every shot (see `../../shared/directing.md` §0).

## Numbers
| | |
|---|---|
| Shot length | in bars: verses 2–4 bars per shot, chorus 1–2 bars, drops on every beat for ≤ 4 bars |
| Camera | moves locked to tempo: a push lasts exactly 1, 2 or 4 bars; sways at half-time |
| Animation | type and graphics hit on beats; secondary motion on off-beats |
| Effects max | level 2 in verses, level 3–4 in choruses and drops |
| Transitions | cut on downbeats, flash on accents, wipe on bar 1 of a section, dissolve in the bridge |
| Light | colour changes per section; brightness pulses on kick (≤ 10%) |

## Synchronisation
1. Beat grid: tempo and downbeats (`../../scripts/analyze.py` estimate, confirmed by ear).
2. Lyric timing: each line's start and end (forced alignment or tapped by hand into `lyrics.json`).
3. Song map (G4): section · bars · lyric lines · energy 1–5 · visual idea · colour.
4. Scene changes on section boundaries; cuts on downbeats; word pops on syllables.
5. Chorus has a signature look that returns every time.

## Lyric typography
One line at a time (two max), current word highlighted, ≥ 2 s per line, centred or on a consistent
baseline; type animation follows syllable timing; never cover faces or the main character.

## Styles
`kinetic-type` (hype, rap, EDM) · `neon-arcade` (synthwave, electronic) · `anime-cel` (emotional, J-pop style) ·
`ink-river`, `wave-print` (traditional, calm) · `stained-window` (devotional) · `kalamkari-scroll`,
`temple-relief` (Indian devotional and folk) · any kids style for kids songs.

Full procedure: `../../shared/music-video.md`.

## Packaging
Title: `<Song> · <Artist> (Official Lyric Video)`. Description: full lyrics, credits, rights.
Shorts: the chorus hook with the strongest lyric as on-screen text.

## Checklist
- [ ] ≥ 80% of cuts on beats; section changes on bar 1
- [ ] Lyrics timed within 2 frames, readable, never over faces
- [ ] Chorus look recurs consistently
- [ ] Rights to the song confirmed (our own or licensed)
