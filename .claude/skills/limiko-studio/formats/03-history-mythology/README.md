# 4 · History and mythology

**Promise:** the great stories of the past (real events, legends and myths) told cinematically
and accurately, in art styles that come from the cultures themselves.
**Files here:** `styles.md` · `storytelling.md` (structure, accuracy, respect) · `packaging.md` · this playbook.

## Settings
| | Default |
|---|---|
| Formats | Short 45–60 s · story 5–12 min · documentary 12–25 min · series episodes |
| Size | 1920×1080 (cinematic, optional 2.39:1 letterbox) · 1080×1920 Shorts |
| Route | A · code-drawn in a culture-matched style; public-domain artworks and maps; B for rare photoreal shots |
| Voice | storyteller: deep, calm, 140–155 wpm, pauses before turns; `A-kokoro` or a licensed voice |
| Music | epic or traditional: drums, low strings, choir, regional instruments (veena, flute, shehnai, duduk, lyre, taiko) |
| Pace | 2.5–4 s shots; slow pushes and pans over painted scenes; faster only in battles |
| Engines | `E-film`, `E-html`, `D-blender-mcp` for 3D maps, `G-uncopyright` (public-domain art and maps) |

## Video types
| Type | Structure |
|---|---|
| **Myth retelling** | cold open on the most dramatic image → the world before → the hero and the desire → the trial → the turn (god, curse, gift, battle) → the ending → what the story means / where it's told today |
| **History event** | the moment it all changed (hook) → rewind: the world, the people → rising tension, dated → the event → consequences → "and that's why today…" |
| **Biography** | a defining moment → childhood → rise → crisis → legacy, with dates on screen |
| **Mystery of the past** | the unexplained thing → what we know (evidence) → theories, each labelled as a theory → the most accepted answer |
| **Versus / comparison** | two empires, gods, warriors or versions of a myth side by side |
| **Short** | one scene, one twist: "the moment <hero> <did X>" |

## Procedure
1. **Research** (see `storytelling.md`): sources for history; named texts and versions for myths.
2. **Logline and beat sheet** (5–8 beats), with dates or text references per beat.
3. **Pick the style** matching the culture and era (`styles.md`). Never mix two cultures' art in one scene.
4. **Script** as a storyteller: present tense for drama, short sentences, one image per sentence.
5. **Storyboard** 12–40 shots: wide establishing → medium → close-up on faces and objects of power.
6. **Maps**: open with where and when (a map shot with the year) for every history video.
7. **Sound design first**: ambience bed (wind, temple bells, battlefield, river), then music cues on the turn.
8. **Build**, grade warm (parchment, gold, deep red) or cold (stone, steel, night) to the story's mood.
9. **Check** with the `storytelling.md` checklist.
10. **Package** with `packaging.md`; sources and text references in the description.

## Topic wells
Indian epics and Puranas · Telugu and South Indian kingdoms (Satavahanas, Kakatiyas, Vijayanagara) ·
Greek, Norse, Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Japanese, Chinese myths · ancient wonders · lost cities ·
great battles · inventions of the ancient world · legendary rulers · "the day <empire> fell".
