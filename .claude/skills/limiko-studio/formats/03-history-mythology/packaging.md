# History and mythology packaging

General rules: `../../shared/packaging.md`.

## Hooks (first 2–3 s)
| Pattern | Example shape |
|---|---|
| Dramatic moment | "<Year>. <Place>. <One shocking event>." over the epic image |
| Untold side | "Everyone knows <hero>. Few know what happened <after/before>." |
| Curse / prophecy | "It was said that <prophecy>. It came true." |
| Mystery | "Nobody knows how <ancient people> <did impossible thing>." |
| Versus | "<God/Empire A> vs <God/Empire B>: who really <won/was stronger>?" |
| One-line story | "The day <hero> <did X> changed <world> forever." |
| Still with us | "You say this word every day. It comes from a <N>-year-old story." |

## Titles
- `The Untold Story of <Name>` · `How <Empire> Fell in <N> Days` · `<Hero> and the <Object/Curse>`
- `Why <God> <Did Surprising Thing> | <Text> Explained` · `The Mystery of <Place>`
- `<Myth> Explained in <N> Minutes` · `<Empire> vs <Empire>`
- Proper names spelled the common search way; the text's name in the title for myths (e.g. Mahabharata).

## Thumbnail
Epic silhouette or the hero's face in the film's style, a glowing object of power, 1–3 words
("THE CURSE", "1 DAY LEFT"). Sacred figures always depicted with dignity.

## Description template
```
<The story in one line, with names and the text or era.>
<What the video covers, second search phrase.>

⏱ 00:00 <cold open> · 01:10 <chapter> …

📜 Sources / texts:
- <Text, version or translation>
- <Book, author, year> / <museum or encyclopaedia link>

Art: original illustrations in <style>; maps drawn for <year>.
#mythology #<culture>mythology #history #<hero>
```

## Tag sets (8–12)
History: `<event>, <empire> history, history of <place>, ancient history, history documentary, <ruler>,
<year/century>, world history, history explained, <series name>`
Mythology: `<myth/text>, <culture> mythology, <hero/god> story, mythology explained, <text> story,
ancient myths, legends, epic story, <series name>`
