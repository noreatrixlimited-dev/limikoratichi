# 06 · Documentary and real-world stories: direction profile

**Identity:** continuous, grounded storytelling. Real locations, maps, archival-style imagery,
animated timelines, professional narration. Transitions feel natural; the story never stops for an effect.

## Numbers
| | |
|---|---|
| Median shot | 4.5 s (3–9 s) |
| Camera | establishing wide → medium → close-up grammar; slow tracking and aerial moves; Ken Burns pans over archival stills (≤ 10% scale change) |
| Effects max | level 2 (animated maps, timelines, labels, subtle film texture on archival) |
| Transitions | straight cut, J/L-cuts, cross-dissolve for time passing, map/timeline transitions, match cut |
| Sound | location ambience, measured narration (145–160 wpm), restrained score, interview-style pauses; −16 LUFS |

## Building blocks
- **Establishing shots**: every new location opens with a wide plus a map pin and a lower-third (place, year).
- **Archival treatment**: public-domain photos and documents with a slow push, paper texture, date stamp.
- **Animated timeline**: a horizontal line that scrolls; events pop in as narrated; the current year highlighted.
- **Maps**: real geography, borders correct for the year, route lines draw at the speed of the story.
- **Data**: simple charts in the film's palette, sourced.
- **Real footage**: the user's own, licensed, or public-domain; AI clips only for reconstructions, labelled "reconstruction".

## Styles
| id | Look | For |
|---|---|---|
| `archive-desk` | photos, letters and maps on a desk, slow camera across them | historical real stories |
| `satellite-pass` | top-down map tiles, coordinates, zoom from orbit | places, disasters, journeys |
| `newsreel-1920` | sepia grain, title cards | early 1900s stories |
| `clean-doc` | real footage + minimal white lower-thirds and thin-line maps | modern documentaries |
| `data-story` | bold charts, one highlight colour | issue-driven stories |
| `parchment-map` | aged map with campaign arrows | travel and conquest |

## Special planning (G4)
Sequence list: for each chapter, establishing → subject → detail → context (map/timeline/data) → payoff,
with the source for each claim and each image's licence.

## Packaging
Hooks: the human moment ("She had 40 minutes to decide"), the place and year, the consequence.
Titles: `The True Story of <X>`, `How <Event> Changed <Place>`, `<Place>: The <N> Days That…`.
Description lists sources and image credits.

## Checklist
- [ ] Every claim and image sourced and licensed; reconstructions labelled
- [ ] Each new place has establishing shot + map + lower-third
- [ ] Narration and picture agree; no "wallpaper" visuals
- [ ] Natural transitions only; no flashy effects
