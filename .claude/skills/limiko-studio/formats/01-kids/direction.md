# 01 · Kids: direction profile

**Identity:** slow, smooth, colourful, friendly. A warm picture book that moves. The child always
knows where to look and has time to understand and repeat.

## Numbers
| | |
|---|---|
| Median shot | 4 s (3–6 s); learning items held ≥ 4 s |
| Camera | very slow: push-ins ≤ 8% over a shot, pans ≤ 8% of width per second |
| Animation | on twos, soft ease, squash and stretch 15–25%, holds ≥ 16 frames |
| Effects max | level 1 (soft glow, gentle sparkles, floating particles) |
| Transitions | straight cut, cross-dissolve (18 f), page turn, iris wipe on the hero. Nothing else |
| Sound | soft effects (pop, boing, chime, page), music 90–120 bpm major key, −16 LUFS |
| Colour | 4–6 saturated friendly colours + cream; no pure black backgrounds |

## Always
- Characters: approved spec, identical in every shot; simple expressions (happy, surprised, sad, thinking, sleepy).
- Clear actions: one action per shot, staged in profile or facing camera, large on screen.
- Background life: clouds drift, flowers sway, birds hop. Gentle, never busy.
- Words to learn appear as large text, are said twice, and stay on screen while said.
- A visual "anchor" (the hero) on screen most of the time.

## Never
- Flashing more than 3×/s, strobe, screen shake, whip pans, crash zooms, glitch, fast cuts.
- Sudden loud sounds, scary faces, darkness, danger shown as fun.
- Clutter: more than 3 moving things at once besides the background.

## Camera rules (kids)
The camera always moves, but slowly and calmly, so a child never loses the subject.
| Situation | Camera move | Numbers |
|---|---|---|
| Every shot (default) | gentle drift or slow push-in | zoom ≤ 8% over the shot; pan ≤ 8% of width per second; never fully static |
| Hero talks or sings | slow push-in towards the face | 100 → 106% over 3–5 s, ease in-out |
| New place | slow pan or slow pull-out to show it | 4–6 s, constant speed |
| Learning item (word, number, colour) | slow push-in onto the item, then hold the move gently | item stays fully in frame ≥ 4 s |
| Storybook scenes | parallax layers under a slow pan | foreground 1.6×, middle 1×, background 0.4× |
| Song chorus | soft sway with the beat | ≤ 2% side-to-side |
| Never | shake, whip pans, crash zooms, fast orbits, dutch angles | |

## Special planning (G4)
Beat sheet with **repetition built in**: what is repeated (word, song line, action) and how many times.
Mark the "pause and answer" moments (3 s holds) and where the child is invited to join in.
